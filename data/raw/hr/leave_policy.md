# Employee Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should submit planned leave requests through the HR portal.

## Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Leave Approval

Planned leave should normally be requested at least three working days in advance.

The manager reviews the request before HR records the approved leave.

## Emergency Leave

Employees may contact their manager and HR directly when an emergency prevents them from submitting a normal leave request.
