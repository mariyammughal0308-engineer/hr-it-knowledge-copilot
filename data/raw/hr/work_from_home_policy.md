# Work From Home Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains the company's work-from-home rules and eligibility requirements.

## Remote Work Allowance

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

Employees must coordinate their remote work schedule with their manager.

## Request Process

Employees must submit a work-from-home request through the HR portal at least one business day before the requested remote-work day.

The employee's manager must approve the request before the remote-work day.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may request remote work unless their role requires regular physical presence at the company office.

## Exceptions

Employees who need additional remote-work days because of exceptional circumstances should contact HR for approval.
