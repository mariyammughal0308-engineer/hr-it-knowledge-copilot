# VPN Password Reset Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how employees can reset their corporate VPN password.

## Password Reset

If your VPN password has expired, open the IT Service Portal.

Select "VPN Password Reset" from the available services.

Enter your company username and follow the verification instructions.

After completing the verification process, create a new password according to the company's password requirements.

## VPN Connection

After changing the password, disconnect the VPN client and sign in again using the new password.

If the VPN still does not connect, restart the VPN client and verify that your internet connection is working.

## Technical Support

If the password reset process fails, create an IT support ticket through the IT Service Portal.

Include the error message and your computer's hostname in the ticket.
