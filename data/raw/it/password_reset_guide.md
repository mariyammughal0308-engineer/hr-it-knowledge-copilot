# Corporate Password Reset Guide

## Forgotten Password

Employees who have forgotten their corporate password should use the Password Reset option on the company identity portal.

## Reset Process

Open the company identity portal.

Select "Forgot Password".

Enter your company username.

Complete the identity verification process.

Create a new password that satisfies the company's password requirements.

## Account Lockout

If your account has been locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts, contact the IT Service Desk.
